Frequently asked questions
How do I send money to another PNB customer?
From your dashboard, tap To PNB Account. Enter the recipient's 10-digit account number and the amount. You'll confirm with your 4-digit PIN and a transfer code. A 3% fee applies.
How do I change my transaction PIN?
Go to Profile → Settings → Change transaction PIN. Enter your current PIN, then your new 4-digit PIN twice.
Is my money insured?
Yes. Deposits with PNB are insured up to ₱500,000 per depositor by the Philippine Deposit Insurance Corporation (PDIC).
